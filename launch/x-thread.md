## Post 1: Hook

Swarmie v2 is live.

Paste a pitch. A panel of AI reviewers reads it like Reddit, HN and Product Hunt would.

You get a decision memo, not a score: what's working, the risk you missed, and what to test this week.

## Post 2: What changed

V1 was blunt for the sake of it. Easy to dismiss.

V2 opens with what's working, then names the hidden risks, shows the reactions behind every claim, and ends with fixes to try.

Not flattering. Not cruel. Useful.

[IMAGE: Decision memo top: verdict, "What's working", "Hidden risks".]

## Post 3: How it works

Up to 500 reviewers, each with a different background and bias.

Most scroll past without a word, like a real feed. Silence costs zero tokens.

The ones who stop object, ask, or upvote. The memo cites them as exhibits.

[IMAGE: Watching screen mid-run, reviewer count ticking up. GIF preferred.]

## Post 4: Three review types

Validate: does the market care?
Investor: would a partner take the meeting? Upload your deck.
Launch: how will HN, PH and Reddit react? Go / sharpen / hold.

## Post 5: How I keep it honest

Every memo change runs against trap pitches:

→ a weak pitch that baits flattery
→ a strong one that baits harshness
→ one with a risk I planted

The memo has to pass all three.
