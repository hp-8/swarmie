## Post 2 — How it works (swarm visual moment)

Paste a pitch. 100–500 AI personas react.

Skeptics, lurkers, fans, trolls. A chunk of the swarm scrolls past without saying a word — because real audiences do that too, and modeling silence is free.

The ones who stop: post objections, ask hard questions, write snark.

## Post 3 — The decision brief (not a score)

You don't get a number.

You get:
→ Verdict (ship / sharpen / wrong audience / kill)
→ The single next action
→ Top objections + the exact question to ask 5 real users
→ Kill-criteria + fix
→ Why the silent majority scrolled past
